Each contributor must personally affirm acceptance of CLA version 1.0
in the pull request (or provide another recorded written agreement)
before their contribution is merged. A maintainer must verify that all
contributors have agreed; submission alone, a checkbox checked by
someone else, or a Git sign-off is not sufficient. Record the agreed
version and contributor identity with the PR. Third-party material must
retain its applicable notices and be compatible with the intended use.

Do not merge a contribution until necessary rights are confirmed.
